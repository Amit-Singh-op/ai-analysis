## 10 Documented Demo Questions

### 1. Critical Temperature Violations
- **Prompt:** `"Which consignments had critical temperature violations yesterday?"`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `getThresholdViolations` or `getRecentEvents`
- **Arguments:** `{ "level": "CRITICAL", "from": <anchor - 86400>, "to": <anchor> }`
- **Expected Fact:** Consignment `CSG-1001` (`CSG-PHARMA-1001`) with device `DEV-002` experienced multiple critical excursions exceeding the 8.0°C maximum limit, reaching up to ~11.8°C.

### 2. Device Telemetry Trend
- **Prompt:** `"Show the temperature and humidity trend for device DEV-002 over the last 24 hours."`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `getTelemetry`
- **Arguments:** `{ "deviceUid": "DEV-002", "from": <anchor - 86400>, "to": <anchor> }`
- **Expected Fact:** Returns chronological telemetry readings at 5-minute intervals. Identifies normal temperatures (~5°C) followed by an elevation up to ~11.8°C during the excursion period, with humidity between 40% and 55%.

### 3. Low Battery and Stale Reporting Devices
- **Prompt:** `"Which devices have low battery or have stopped reporting?"`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `getLowBatteryDevices` and/or `getStaleDevices`
- **Arguments:** `{ "threshold": 20 }` and `{ "staleAfterSeconds": 7200 }`
- **Expected Fact:** 
  - `DEV-003` is flagged with low battery (11.5%, below 20% threshold).
  - `DEV-004` is flagged as STALE (has not sent pings for over 36 hours).

### 4. Consignment Comparison
- **Prompt:** `"Compare average temperature across consignments 1001 and 1002."`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `compareConsignments`
- **Arguments:** `{ "consignmentUids": [1001, 1002], "from": <anchor - 86400>, "to": <anchor> }`
- **Expected Fact:** 
  - `CSG-1001`: Average temp ~6.5°C to 7.5°C, with multiple critical violation events.
  - `CSG-1002`: Average temp ~4.3°C to 4.8°C, with 0 violations (ideal cold chain).

### 5. Consignment Critical Events Summary
- **Prompt:** `"Summarize critical events for consignment 1001."`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `getRecentEvents` or `getTemperatureSummary`
- **Arguments:** `{ "consignmentUid": 1001, "level": "CRITICAL" }`
- **Expected Fact:** Reports `TEMPERATURE_VIOLATION` events with recorded temperatures > 8.0°C and severity labeled `CRITICAL`.

### 6. Fleet Filtering by Device Type
- **Prompt:** `"Which devices are registered as COLD_CHAIN_LOGGER?"`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `listDevices`
- **Arguments:** `{ "deviceType": "COLD_CHAIN" }`
- **Expected Fact:** Returns the list of cold-chain loggers (`DEV-001`, `DEV-005`, `DEV-009`, etc.) without fabricating non-existent devices.

### 7. Consignment Routing & Schedule
- **Prompt:** `"List consignments routed from Frankfurt to Singapore."`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `listConsignments`
- **Arguments:** `{}`
- **Expected Fact:** Identifies `CSG-1002` (`CSG-VACCINE-1002`, origin: Frankfurt, Germany, destination: Singapore).

### 8. Temperature Summary for a Shipment
- **Prompt:** `"What was the minimum, maximum, and average temperature for consignment 1002?"`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `getTemperatureSummary`
- **Arguments:** `{ "consignmentUid": 1002, "from": ..., "to": ... }`
- **Expected Fact:** Minimum ~3.5°C, maximum ~5.5°C, average ~4.5°C. Reports 0 null readings and 0 violations.

### 9. Humidity Anomalies and Shock Detection
- **Prompt:** `"Did any device detect high humidity or shock?"`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `getHumidityAnomalies` or `getRecentEvents`
- **Arguments:** `{ "threshold": 80 }`
- **Expected Fact:** `DEV-005` recorded high humidity exceeding 80% (up to 89%) and logged a critical `SHOCK_DETECTED` event with acceleration at 1850 milli-g.

### 10. Device Status & Health Check
- **Prompt:** `"What is the status of device DEV-004?"`
- **Approved Tool Called:** `getStaleDevices` or `listDevices`
- **Arguments:** `{ "asOf": ... }`
- **Expected Fact:** `DEV-004` is offline / STALE, having sent no pings in over 36 hours.
